How It Works

1. Invoke LaunchBar
Press Command-Space to open the input window instantly.

2. Type Abbreviation
Enter a short, natural abbreviation—like "sf" for Safari or "em john" for emailing John.

3. Select and Act
Best matches appear; hit Return to open, or use arrows for actions like browsing or services.

4. Customize as Needed
Refine rules, templates, and integrations for your exact workflow—evolves with you.
